# PSYCHOLOGICAL_SALIENCE_FRAMEWORK.md

## Status
Canonical, evolvable.

---

## Purpose
Define what psychological information is **most important to preserve and retrieve** for advice quality over decades.

This framework:
- guides memory retention
- guides retrieval priority
- informs compaction decisions

---

## Salience Criteria (Practitioner-Grounded)

A psychological fact or pattern is high-salience if it satisfies multiple criteria below.

### 1. Formative Origin
- Emerges early in life
- Shaped identity or worldview

### 2. Cross-Domain Impact
- Influences multiple life domains (work, relationships, self-concept)

### 3. Recurrence
- Appears repeatedly over time
- Not tied to a single episode

### 4. Explanatory Power
- Explains otherwise confusing behaviors or decisions

### 5. Resistance to Change
- Persists despite effort, insight, or intention

### 6. Advice Relevance
- Directly alters what advice would be appropriate

---

## Usage Notes

- Salience is **graded**, not binary.
- Confidence in salience assignment must be tracked.
- The framework is expected to evolve with experience.
